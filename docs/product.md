# StreamBrew

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Streamers collecting audience support and choosing videos to watch on stream.
Viewers can consult a streamer's public video queue.

## Product Purpose

Bring donations and chat from multiple platforms into one workspace for streamers.
Manage video queues from donation links and manual additions, display donation
alerts in OBS, and forward a live stream to multiple destinations.

## Operating Context

The application includes a dashboard, donations, video queue, multichat, alerts,
integrations, settings, public queues, multistream, and browser-source chat overlays.
Streamers need legible messages and predictable controls during live broadcasts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve existing routes and functionality during the redesign. Follow the [domain glossary](glossary.md)
for terminology, [design guide](engineering/design.md) for interface conventions,
and the domain guides for behavior. Support Russian and English, light and dark
themes, desktop and mobile web. Keep decorative artwork separate from controls.

## Brand Commitments

The coffee-and-cosmos direction, emotional brief, and interface conventions are
maintained in [design guide](engineering/design.md#original-associations).

## Evidence on Hand

Product facts above come from the user's AGENTS.md and the existing route tree.
Brand intent comes from the user's request and the original associations preserved
in the design guide. Existing brand
assets are in apps/web/assets. No new marketing claims or performance figures

## Product Principles

- Make waiting and incoming support feel alive.
- Keep live-stream tasks legible and quick.
- Carry one coherent coffee-cosmic world across every surface.
- Preserve donation and video semantics.
